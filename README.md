# Sephora Black Friday: How to Stack Cashback on Cyber Week Orders Without Voiding It

Most people searching for Sephora Black Friday want one of three things: the dates, the actual discount numbers, or a way to pay less than the sale price. The first two are easy enough to find. The third one is where most guides stop short, usually right after telling you to "check the sale section."

Here's the thing worth knowing up front: Sephora's Black Friday event is not a one-day event, and the discount you see at checkout is not the lowest price available on that order. There's a second layer most shoppers never activate — and a set of rules that quietly cancels it if you do the wrong thing at checkout.

## What Sephora's Black Friday sale actually is

Sephora doesn't call it Black Friday. It calls it Cyber Week, and it runs for roughly eight days rather than the traditional single Friday.

In 2025 the window ran from Saturday, November 22 through Thursday, December 4 [3]. The year before it started November 25 and ended December 2 [6]. Notice the gap: one source had the 2025 sale opening on the 22nd while another predicted the 24th [6]. That disagreement is useful information in itself — the exact start date moves, so the only reliable source for the current window is Sephora's own site and app.

A few structural details that matter:

- It runs online, in stores, and at Sephora locations inside Kohl's (including Kohl's website) [6].
- Thanksgiving Day is online-only. Sephora stores are typically closed that day [3].
- Stores open early on Black Friday itself, sometimes as early as 6 a.m. [3] — relevant if you want to swatch a fragrance before buying it, which is not something a product page does well.

Sephora also runs the same event under different mechanics outside the US. Its Italian arm advertised a flat **25% off skincare, haircare and fragrance** during its 2025 Black Friday week [4], while French coverage of earlier editions showed 20–30% off fragrances and category-level cuts rather than US-style rotating daily brands [5]. If you're shopping a European Sephora, expect a flatter, category-wide discount instead of a daily brand rotation.

## What actually gets discounted

Sephora's Cyber Week works on two tracks that run at the same time.

**Rotating daily brand deals.** Each day of the week, a specific brand or two gets a deeper cut, historically around 30%. Past rotations included Urban Decay, Tarte, Fenty Beauty, Kiehl's, Clinique, Youth To The People and Peter Thomas Roth [6]. These are 24-hour windows, so the deal you planned to buy tomorrow may be gone tonight.

**Ongoing event-wide offers.** These run the length of the sale rather than a single day. In 2024 that included up to 50% off select fragrances, plus a free sample bag with online orders of $90 or more [6]. GXVE eyeshadow palettes dropped to $14 and Fenty's Icon Velvet Liquid Lipstick hit 50% off during the same period [1].

For a sense of scale, DataWeave monitored pricing across Sephora, Ulta, Walmart, Target and Amazon during Black Friday 2024 and measured an average discount of **38.1%** at Sephora, against 45% at Ulta [2]. Sephora's average sits above Walmart's, but it trails Ulta on headline discount depth — a reminder that "Black Friday" at Sephora means selective deep cuts, not a storewide percentage.

Three policy details that catch people out:

> Sephora does **not** price match Black Friday deals, and it does **not** offer rain checks. Every deal is while supplies last [6].

Returns are the one area that's forgiving. Unused or gently used items can go back within 30 days for a refund to your original payment method; after 30 days you get store credit instead [6].

## The part most Black Friday guides skip: Black Friday isn't Sephora's deepest discount

This is worth sitting with for a second, because it changes how you should plan.

Sephora runs two sitewide **Savings Events** a year through its Beauty Insider program — one around April, one around November ahead of the holidays. During those events the *entire* catalog goes on sale at a tiered percentage off, including prestige brands that almost never get a standalone discount outside of gift-with-purchase promotions. The event discount is funded by Sephora and applied to the order total [7].

ShopBack's own beauty editorial puts it bluntly: Sephora is one of the few large US retailers where the best prices are not at Black Friday, because the Savings Events are when the full assortment carries a meaningful percentage discount [7].

In practice this means:

- If the thing you want lands in a Cyber Week daily deal, buy it then. A 30% brand deal is deeper than a tier-dependent event discount.
- If the thing you want never appears in a daily deal — a specific foundation shade, a signature fragrance, a tool — the Savings Event is probably the better window.
- Either way, the discount rate on a Savings Event depends on your Beauty Insider tier, which is based on calendar-year spend and resets annually [7].

## Beauty Insider tiers, in one table

Beauty Insider is free to join, and all three tiers get access to the Savings Events and can activate cashback on the discounted order [7]. What changes is the percentage.

| Tier | Annual spend | Savings Event discount | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Insider | First year, or under $350 | Smallest sitewide discount | Free |
| VIB | $350 – $999 | Mid-tier discount | Free |
| Rouge | $1,000+ in a calendar year | Deepest sitewide discount | Free |

Event discounts broadly land in the **10–20%** range depending on tier, with Rouge getting the largest cut [7]. Because tiers reset each calendar year, a year where you're planning a large skincare or fragrance purchase is worth tracking against the Rouge threshold.

One myth to drop: Beauty Insider does not get early or exclusive access to Black Friday deals [6]. Free shipping for members is a permanent benefit, not a Black Friday perk [6]. If your purchase is small, Sephora at Kohl's is the one place you can genuinely double up — you earn Kohl's Rewards *and* Beauty Insider points on the same order [6].

## How cashback actually moves from "pending" to money

Cashback isn't instant, and that surprises people more than the percentage does.

1. **Tracked** — the purchase shows up against your account, usually within 48 hours. If nothing appears, you file a missing cashback claim from inside the account with the order number, date and amount [11].
2. **Pending** — the retailer holds it while the return and cancellation window is open, so cancellations and partial returns can adjust the amount. Most retail orders confirm within 30–90 days [12]. The timeline on ShopBack's own Sephora guide lands at roughly 90 days [7].
3. **Confirmed** — the merchant has verified the order was kept and paid the commission. The amount is now fixed [13].
4. **Withdrawable** — you transfer it out to a linked bank account or PayPal. It's real money, not store credit or points [12].

On the payout side: you can withdraw once confirmed cashback reaches **$10**, the daily withdrawal cap is **$300**, and transfers typically land within 10 calendar days, though the terms allow up to 30 since payouts are processed in batches [14]. Confirmed balances don't expire — there's no pressure to withdraw early [12].

## The rules that silently void cashback on a Sephora order

This is where most people lose the money they already earned. The terms listed on ShopBack's Sephora pages are unusually specific, and most of them bite at exactly the moment you're rushing a Black Friday checkout.

- **Featured codes only.** A promo code that isn't listed on the platform can void cashback for the whole order. Codes featured there stack; a code from another coupon site usually doesn't. If you plan to stack a Black Friday code, confirm it's the featured one first.
- **Clear your cart before clicking through.** Items already sitting in your Sephora cart when you arrive from the cashback link won't be tracked.
- **Don't order through the Sephora app.** App purchases are excluded. Use the web route.
- **Skip gift cards.** Buying gift cards earns nothing, and paying with a gift card or store credit is also excluded.
- **Brand exclusions apply.** Dyson, GHD, Cloud Nine, Lelo, Mermade Hair, Shark Beauty and Smile Makers are excluded, and The Ordinary is carved out in some markets. Fees, taxes, shipping and delivery are never included.
- **Accept all cookies.** The retailer can only confirm a transaction if the cookies on its pages are accepted.
- **No adblockers, VPNs, or other coupon extensions.** Anything intercepting the click-through can break tracking.
- **The extension won't save you here.** The Sephora terms on ShopBack's pages state that extension tracking doesn't work for this merchant — you have to click through from the platform.

Returned, cancelled, refunded or exchanged orders are ineligible outright; partial returns are pro-rated as an adjustment.

## Questions that come up every year

**Does Sephora's Black Friday sale work in stores?** Yes — online, in stores, and at Sephora inside Kohl's. Some deals are channel-exclusive, though in-store pickup can usually bridge that gap [3].

**Is the cashback rate the same on Black Friday?** Not necessarily. Cashback is a percentage of what you pay after discounts, so a deeper sale price means a smaller cashback total on the same item — but a lower net price than buying outside the sale. Some platforms also run temporary rate boosts during major sale events, which is why checking the live rate on the day beats assuming.

**Can I use a Black Friday promo code and still earn cashback?** Only if that specific code is the featured one on the cashback platform's merchant page. A code from anywhere else can void the whole order's cashback.

**How long until the money is actually mine?** Tracked in about two days, confirmed after the return window closes — around 90 days for Sephora — then withdrawable to a bank account or PayPal [7][12].
